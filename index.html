Full Description

Charlie Barber outlines 5 proven AI services: (1) AI Lead Reactivation and Outreach - automated DM/email to old leads using MessengerFlow, AutoLeads, Instantly.ai; (2) AI Content Operator - avatar content with HeyGen + Descript + ManyChat + trial reels for 5000 new followers/month; (3) RevOps - connecting marketing/sales/fulfillment into one AI pipeline, diagnosing 3 leak points (lead-to-booking, call-to-close, close-to-onboarding); (4) SaaS Retention and Churn Reduction - automated follow-up messaging to increase LTV; (5) AI Sales and Conversion Agent - AI reviews sales calls/DMs/emails to identify objections and patterns, improving close rates 10%+.

Pain Point

Missing 30K-100K/year from unfollowed leads. Content inconsistency. Revenue leaking between stages. Customer churn. 10% close rate improvement at 10K/deal = 100K extra.

Pricing Justification

Reactivation: 30-100K unused in old leads. Content: replaces 2K/month human. RevOps: fewer leaks = more revenue. Retention: increases LTGP. Sales: 10% on 100 deals at 10K = 100K extra.

Notion pipeline. RevOps: 1. Diagnostic call. 2. ID worst leak. 3. Build automation. Sales: 1. Connect Fireflies. 2. N8N + LLM analysis. 3. Summarize calls. 4. Weekly reports + Slack recaps.

Tools Stack

MessengerFlow - Facebook DMs. AutoLeads.app - Instagram outreach. Instantly.ai - cold email. LA10 - content research. HeyGen - avatar cloning. Descript - AI video editing. Client Acquisition

Cold email campaigns. School.com outreach with Loom VSSL (few minutes). Funnel: outreach > Loom > booked call > close.

Outreach Script

Framework: reach out on School.com, send short Loom VSSL focused on end results not mechanisms. Always sell the destination not the journey.

Sales Process

1. Outreach on School.com or cold email. 2. Short Loom VSSL on end results. 3. Funnel to booked call. 4. Act as doctor - diagnostic questions. 5. ID specific problem. 6. Mold offer to their problems. 7. Present ROI-focused solution. 8. Close.

Objection Handling

Act as doctor - morally wrong to prescribe without diagnosing. Ask questions first. Lead with value. Sell destination not journey. Show personal proof (I pay 2K/month for this myself).

Mistake Warning

Not knowing niche deeply enough. Not looking the part on profiles. Selling mechanisms instead of outcomes. Trying everything instead of diagnosing ONE leak. Not testing hooks before posting.

Scalability

Start reactivation (lowest barrier). Build to RevOps. Add content operator. Layer sales analysis for high-ticket. Each service compounds.
